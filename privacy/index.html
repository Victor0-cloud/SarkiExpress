Sarki Express Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy covers the Sarki Express game played at www.sarkiexpress.fun and the Sarki Express Android app.

The short version

Sarki Express does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

What the game saves

The game keeps one save file on your own device. It holds your game progress: coins, score, upgrades and shields you own, finished trips and missions, whether you have seen the intro, your sound setting, your riding mode, and the rider name shown in the game. In the browser this is stored in your browser's local storage for this site. On Android it is stored in the app's private storage.

This save never leaves your device. We cannot see it.

Network use

The Android app does not request the internet permission and makes no network connections. On the web, your browser downloads the game files from our host (Vercel). Like any web host, Vercel may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and operation. We do not use those logs to track you.

Sharing

We do not sell or share any data with anyone, because the game does not collect any.

Deleting your data

On Android, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. On the web, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Either removes your save completely.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

Questions: sales@sarkiexpress.fun
